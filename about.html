Our story

Grounded in one neighbourhood, working across NCR

NCR Realty works out of Safdarjung Enclave in New Delhi — a part of the city where property boundaries, colony layouts and paperwork details are learned by walking the lanes, not just reading listings.

That local grounding carries into every enquiry we take on, whether it's a flat down the road in Saket or a plot across the border in Gurgaon or Noida. We work as a small, direct team — one point of contact from the first phone call through to the final registry appointment.

No hand-offs between departments. No listings that turn out to be already sold. If a property doesn't check out on a site visit, we say so before you make the trip yourself.
