NCR Reality — Services

Four services, one point of contact

Whatever stage you're at — buying, selling, renting or evaluating — we stay involved from the first call to the final signature.

01 / Buying

Residential Sales

We shortlist apartments, builder floors and independent houses against your actual budget and family needs, not just what's available — and walk every site with you before you decide.

  • Shortlisting by budget, locality and size
  • Accompanied site visits
  • Price and title verification
  • Support through to registry
02 / Renting

Rentals & Leasing

For tenants and owners alike — we match verified parties, draft the lease, and stay involved through move-in so nothing gets left as a verbal understanding.

  • Verified tenant/owner matching
  • Lease agreement drafting
  • Move-in coordination
  • Support for early post-move issues
03 / Commercial

Commercial Property

Shops, showrooms and office floors along NCR's commercial corridors — sourced with footfall, access and paperwork checked before you're shown the space.

  • Shop, showroom & office sourcing
  • Footfall and access evaluation
  • Title and NOC checks
  • Lease or sale negotiation
04 / Investing

Investment Advisory

Before you commit capital, we walk you through how a locality compares to others in NCR — on price trajectory, rental demand and what's realistic to expect on resale.

  • Micro-market comparisons across NCR
  • Rental yield and resale outlook
  • Under-construction vs ready-to-move guidance
  • Ongoing portfolio check-ins
